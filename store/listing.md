## Full description
Keep score for your Carcassonne games without pen and paper.

Score keeper for Carcassonne is a simple, fast score pad built for the table. Add your players, give each one their pawn colour, and track points as roads, cities, monasteries and fields are completed.

SCORING THAT KEEPS UP WITH THE GAME
• Tap +1 for each point — quick taps add up into a single entry
• Open the keypad to type a bigger score in one go
• Made a mistake? Enter a negative number, undo the last entry, or remove any entry from the score log
• See who is leading at a glance

PLAYERS AND PAWN COLOURS
• 2 to 8 players
• Choose Yellow, Green, Blue, Black, Red or Pink — or mix any custom colour
• Recent players are one tap away, with the colour they used last time

HISTORY AND ALL-TIME LEADERBOARD
• Every finished game is saved with its date, players, final scores and full score log
• The leaderboard shows wins, games played, win rate, best and average score for each player

MADE FOR GAME NIGHT
• Optional "keep screen awake" setting so the display stays on during the game
• Your game is saved after every change, so nothing is lost if you leave the app
• Light and dark themes

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No ads, no accounts, no internet access, no tracking
• No permissions required — your scores stay on your device
