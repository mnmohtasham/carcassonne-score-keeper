This privacy policy describes how the Android app Score keeper for Carcassonne (package com.mani.carcassonnescorekeeper), published on Google Play by {{DEVELOPER_NAME}}, handles information. In short: the app does not collect, transmit or share any personal or sensitive user data.

Information the app stores

To keep score, the app stores the following on your device only, in the app's private storage:

  • player names and pawn colours you enter,
  • scores, score changes and the date and time of each game,
  • your app settings (for example "Keep screen awake").

This information never leaves your device through the app. The developer has no access to it.

Information collected or shared

None. The app has no internet access, no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no third-party tracking or SDKs that collect data. It requests no Android permissions.

Device backups

If Android backup is turned on for your device, Android may include the app's stored data in your device backup (for example, Google's backup service) under your own account, in the same way as for other apps. This is controlled by your device settings, not by the app.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect data from anyone, including children.

Retention and deletion

Data stays on your device until you delete it. You can delete all finished games in the app under Settings → Clear game history, discard a game in progress from the home screen, or remove all app data by uninstalling the app.
