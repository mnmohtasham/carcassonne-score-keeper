# Privacy Policy — Score keeper for Carcassonne

_Last updated: 12 October 2026_

Score keeper for Carcassonne does not collect, transmit or share any personal data.

- **What is stored:** the player names, pawn colours, scores and dates of games you record, and your app settings. This data is stored only on your device, in the app's private storage.
- **Network:** the app has no internet access and contains no analytics, advertising or tracking.
- **Permissions:** the app requests no permissions.
- **Backups:** if Android backup is enabled on your device, the app's data may be included in your device backup (for example, Google's backup service) under your own account, as with any other app. You can turn this off in your device settings.
- **Deleting data:** use *Settings → Clear game history* in the app, or uninstall the app to remove all of its data.

If you have questions about this policy, contact the developer via the app's store listing.
